step two

Know your file types

One of the quickest checks you can do is look at the file ending.

.package Normal ✓

Most CC: hair, clothes, furniture, poses. If it only holds .package files, that is the expected look.

.ts4script Trusted creators only

Script mods that change gameplay. They run code, so get them from creators you know, and re-check after a game patch.

.exe Never ✗

A mod has no reason to ship a program. Delete it, and don't open it.

Documents / Electronic Arts / The Sims 4 / Mods

Script mods only work if they sit at most one folder deep inside Mods. Keep spare folders outside it and move in only what you want to use.

step three

When something looks wrong

The common trouble spots, what they look like, and what to do next.

(´;ω;`) virus or malware scare

Antivirus flags a file

  • Stop. Don't open it again.
  • Run a full scan with Windows Security or another antivirus.
  • Move the file out of Mods and delete it.
  • Check the creator's page for a warning or a fixed version.
(⊙_⊙)? scam or fake download page

The site acts strange

  • Close pages that redirect you or fill with pop-ups.
  • Skip link shorteners, survey walls, and extra "download managers".
  • Go back to the creator's own page and start again from there.
(╥﹏╥) account or info theft

Someone is in your accounts

  • From a different, clean device, change your passwords.
  • Turn on two-step login for email, Discord, and the EA account.
  • Scan the computer you downloaded on before using it again.
(・・;) game errors or a broken save

Crashes after an update

  • Remove script mods first, then test again.
  • Halve your Mods folder over and over (the 50/50 method) to find the culprit.
  • Tools like Better Exceptions can name the broken file.
  • Restore from your backup if a save won't load.
(・ω・) phishing and social engineering

A message asks for your login

  • Be wary of DMs about giveaways or "verify your account".
  • Check that a Patreon or Tumblr link goes to the real creator.
  • Real creators don't ask for your password.

just in case

Think you downloaded something bad?

  1. Stay calm. Close the game and the browser.
  2. Remove the new file from your Mods folder.
  3. Run a full antivirus scan.
  4. On a clean device, change passwords for email, Discord, and EA.
  5. Turn on two-step login wherever you can.
  6. Tell the creator and the community, so others avoid it.
